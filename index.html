Post Stash

Post Stash is a personal vault for Instagram users who want a better way to organize, categorize, and manage their saved posts. It helps users overcome Instagram's limited organization and filtering capabilities by providing a centralized place to store and manage saved posts.

PROBLEM IT SOLVES
Instagram's Saved feature lacks flexible filtering and organization, making it difficult to manage large collections of saved posts and find unorganized content.

THE MINDSET HOW I APPROACH CODE

My Philosophy

✳

Good software begins with understanding the problem. I value simplicity, thoughtful engineering, and continuous learning over unnecessary complexity and chasing trends.

I.

Code is Communication.

Code is written for humans first and machines second. I value clarity, readability, and maintainability, believing that good code should be easy to understand, not just easy to execute.

II.

Quality Over Shortcuts.

Good engineering is about making thoughtful decisions, not taking the fastest route. I believe in building reliable, maintainable solutions and paying attention to the details that make software dependable.

III.

Growth Through Curiosity.

Technology is constantly evolving, and so should we. I believe in staying curious, learning from mistakes, questioning assumptions, and continuously improving both my skills and the way I approach problems.
